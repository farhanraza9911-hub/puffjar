Puffjar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Developer: Farhan Bashir (Bashir Sons of Apps)

1. About this policy

Puffjar ("the app") is a casual puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we do not collect

Puffjar does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The developer does not run a server that receives personal information from the app.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked skins, settings such as sound, music and volume, your chosen profile name and icon, and your reminder time) is stored locally on your device. It stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This may be used to show personalised or non-personalised ads, to measure performance and to prevent fraud. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ad types used: interstitial ads (for example after a game ends) and rewarded ads (which you choose to watch to earn a reward such as an extra power-up or a daily gift).

5. Consent and your ad choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice later from Settings → Ad privacy options when that option is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one local notification per day on your device at the time you choose. These notifications are created by the app on your device and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your Android system settings.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed to load and show ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service as described above.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional daily reminder.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback in the game, which you can turn off in Settings.

8. Music credits

Background music: "Soft Piano" by Leberch and "Holiday Bells" by Alex Morgan, obtained from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License.

9. Children

Puffjar is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can help.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because the developer does not collect personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For data held by Google (ads), please use your Google account and Android privacy controls.

11. Security

All game assets are bundled inside the app, and the app does not transmit your game progress anywhere. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but we aim to keep the app simple and minimal.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if online features such as a leaderboard are added in the future. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date updated above.
